TeamStation Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

TeamStation is a team management, scoring and game video service for youth and amateur sports, provided through the TeamStation apps for iOS and Android and the website at teamstation.app. TeamStation is operated by Paul Puey ("TeamStation", "we", "us").

This policy explains what information TeamStation collects, how it is used, who processes it on our behalf, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who uses TeamStation, including coaches, team administrators, parents and guardians, players and fans.

The short version

  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own use. There is no advertising, no ad tracking and no third-party analytics in TeamStation.
  • Information leaves TeamStation's own servers only when a service provider has to process it to run a feature for you: hosting, email delivery, maps and place search, or turning spoken scoring calls into text. Those providers act on our behalf and are listed below.
  • Team chat is end-to-end encrypted. Messages and attachments are encrypted on your device. TeamStation's servers store them only in encrypted form and do not have the keys to read them. The one exception is a message you choose to report (see Chat).
  • Players' records are usually created by their coach or guardian. Players are shown by a shortened name, such as "Avery S", to everyone except their guardians, their team's staff and themselves.
  • If you allow it, TeamStation uses your device's approximate location to find nearby events, teams and venues.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. Your name, display name, username, email address or addresses, and a profile picture if you add one.
  • Password. Your password is hashed on your device (Argon2id) before it is sent, and hashed again on our server. We never receive or store your password itself.
  • Team and player information. Teams, seasons and leagues you create or join, and your role in them (for example administrator, coach, scorekeeper, guardian or player). Rosters with player names, jersey numbers, positions, and batting and throwing hands. Links between guardians and the players they look after.
  • Games and events. Schedules, the places and addresses of games and events, RSVPs, lineups, and walk-up songs (song title, artist and clip times).
  • Scoring and statistics. Plays recorded during a game and the statistics calculated from them.
  • Photos. Profile pictures uploaded by you, or by a team administrator for a player on their team.
  • Video. Game video recorded or streamed by a team's cameras, and recordings uploaded to the team.
  • Imported rosters. A team administrator may import a roster from another sports platform. This brings in player names, jersey numbers, batting and throwing hands, team photos, and adults' names, email addresses and guardian relationships.
  • Reports. If you report a chat message, the content of that message (see Chat).

Information collected when you use TeamStation

  • Approximate location. If you allow it, TeamStation uses your device's approximate location (geolocation) to find nearby events, teams and venues. It is sent to our server for that search and is not kept with your account. TeamStation never asks for your precise location or for location in the background.
  • Voice dictation (microphone). Scorekeepers can call plays aloud. The audio is sent from your device to our server and passed to a speech-recognition provider to produce text. We do not store the audio. The resulting text, together with the current game situation (for example who is batting, pitching and on base, by short name and jersey number), is sent to an AI model provider, which turns it into a scoring entry. The microphone is used only while you are dictating.
  • Place search. When you search for a city, venue or address, the text you type is sent from our server to a place-search provider to find matching places.
  • Sign-in and device information. When you sign in, we store a session with your device's name and browser or app type, so you can see and end your sessions. Sessions expire after 30 days (180 days for a linked camera device).
  • Security and abuse prevention. We record your IP address and email address briefly to limit repeated sign-in attempts; these records are deleted when the limit window ends. We keep a log of changes made to teams, rosters and accounts (who made the change and what changed), so that mistakes can be traced and undone.
  • Crash reports. When the app or server hits an error, we record a crash report on an error-tracking server we operate ourselves. It contains your internal TeamStation user ID and technical details of the error. Email addresses, sign-in codes, passwords, tokens and request contents are removed before the report is saved. Crash reports are deleted after 30 days.
  • Cookies. The website uses one cookie, which keeps you signed in. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, your phone number, your date of birth or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

We use information only to:

  • provide TeamStation's features: teams, rosters, schedules, scoring, statistics, video, live streams, chat and invitations;
  • send you account emails such as sign-in codes, sign-in links and invitations;
  • keep accounts and teams secure, prevent abuse, and investigate reports;
  • find and fix errors; and
  • comply with the law.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build profiles of you for marketing.

Who can see your information

  • Anyone, signed in or not, can see a team's public summary: its location, staff, schedule and results.
  • Anyone signed in who is not on the team can also see its roster by short name, without photos, and its scores and list of highlights.
  • Team members see the team's rosters, schedules, scores, statistics, photos and video.
  • A player's full name is visible only to the player, their guardians, and the administrators, coaches, assistants and scorekeepers of a team they play on. Everyone else sees a shortened name.

Chat

TeamStation chat is end-to-end encrypted.

  • Each message and attachment is encrypted on the sender's device with keys that only the devices of the conversation's members hold. TeamStation's servers store and relay only the encrypted form and have no way to decrypt it.
  • Your chat keys are kept in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore), or encrypted in your browser's storage on the web. They are not backed up to TeamStation.
  • TeamStation cannot read your messages or attachments, and does not have the keys to them.

To deliver messages, TeamStation's servers can see which conversations exist, their titles, and which team, season or league they belong to; who is a member of each conversation and when they joined or left; who sent each message and when, its approximate size, and whether it is a message, reaction, edit or deletion; the size of attachments; and read status, pins, mute settings and the names of your linked devices.

Reporting a message. If you report a message, your device sends that one message's content and its key to TeamStation so we can review the report. Only the reported message is revealed; nothing else in the conversation is.

Children and chat. Users under 13 cannot use chat. Direct messages between an adult and a minor follow youth-safety rules: another adult must be included.

Children

TeamStation is used by youth sports teams, so it holds information about children who play on those teams: names, jersey numbers, team photos, statistics and game video.

  • A child's player record is created by their coach, team administrator or guardian, not by the child. A child does not need an account to be on a team.
  • We do not knowingly let a child under 13 create their own account without a parent's or guardian's consent.
  • Player photos are stored privately and shown only through links that expire.
  • A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete their child's information by contacting us. We will verify the request and respond.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information without a parent's consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

  • The apps and website talk to TeamStation over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Live video from a team's camera devices is currently sent to our server over a streaming connection that is not encrypted.
  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, and sign-in tokens and email codes are stored only as hashes.
  • Photos and video are kept in private storage and served only through signed links that expire.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of a breach as the law requires.

How long we keep information

  • Account, team, roster, scoring and video information, and the change log: for as long as your account or team exists.
  • Sign-in sessions: 30 days (180 days for camera devices).
  • Email sign-in codes: 10 minutes.
  • Sign-in rate-limit records: until the limit window ends.
  • Crash reports: 30 days.
  • Replaced video recordings: deleted about a month after being replaced.
  • Dictation audio: not stored.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. You can view and change your name, photo, password and email addresses in the app.
  • Leaving. You can leave a team or step down from a role at any time.
  • Deleting your account. Email us from the address on your account to ask for your account and its information to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep information for legal reasons. Team records you created, such as game scores, may remain with the team without your name.
  • Location and microphone. You can turn location or microphone access off in your device settings at any time; the rest of TeamStation keeps working.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have further rights to access, delete or port your information, or to object to its processing. Contact us to use them; we will not treat you differently for doing so.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app or by email.
